## Modifications

Work on the port started on 2026-10-14. In summary, compared to the NeoForge original:

- The mod loader was changed from NeoForge to Fabric (Fabric Loader and Fabric API, Minecraft 1.21.1).
- The mod id was changed to `framedblocks_fabric`. Worlds and items created with the NeoForge mod are therefore not compatible.
- Rendering was rebuilt on the Fabric Rendering API.
- NeoForge capabilities were replaced by the Fabric Transfer API.
- The integrations with Create, Applied Energistics 2 and Building Gadgets were removed.
- A new logo (pixel art, generated by `tools/logo/generate.mjs`) replaces the original one.

The complete list of changes is the git history of this repository compared with the upstream repository.
